Description
Stop checking pull requests on repeat.

Human Turn watches reviews, CI, and coding agents from your Mac's menu bar, then shows the moment your attention is needed.

See every tracked pull request in one calm view:

- Your turn: review requests, changes requested, failed checks, finished agents, and work that is ready to merge.
- Waiting: work that is still with an agent, CI, or reviewer.
- Unclear: incomplete or ambiguous signals that Human Turn will not guess about.

CONNECT YOUR WORK

Track pull requests across GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket Cloud, and Cursor Origin. Follow coding-agent work from Cursor Cloud Agents and GitHub Copilot alongside the pull requests it affects.

RETURN AT THE RIGHT MOMENT

Human Turn can notify you when responsibility passes back to you, an agent needs input or fails, or a pull request becomes ready to merge. Sensible cooldowns and per-repository controls keep notifications useful instead of noisy.

STAY FOCUSED

Snooze an item for an hour, until tomorrow, or until new commits arrive. Mute repositories, ignore individual items, and sort work by how long it has been waiting. Open the exact pull request, failed check, or agent run when you are ready to act.

HAND OFF REVIEWS

Copy a ready-made /review command for a pull request and paste it into the coding agent you already use. Human Turn does not launch tools, change your code, or require an agent preference.

SEE WHAT CHANGED

Human Turn remembers the revision you last opened and reports whether new commits arrived. On compatible Macs, optional change summaries use Apple Intelligence on device. No code, patches, repository names, account identities, or credentials are sent to a summary server.

BUILT FOR THE MENU BAR

Human Turn is a native macOS app with no Dock clutter. It refreshes quietly in the background, supports keyboard navigation, can launch at login, and keeps the current state close at hand.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Human Turn runs locally on your Mac. Credentials are stored in Keychain and sent only to the services you connect, never to Human Turn servers. The app reads your development activity and does not merge pull requests or modify repositories.

Human Turn requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Intelligence change summaries require macOS 26 or later, an eligible Mac, and Apple Intelligence enabled with its model downloaded.
